# Support SLAs and escalation

## Priorities and first-response targets

| Priority | Definition | Business / Enterprise | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Pulse unavailable or alerting broken | 1 hour, 24/7 | 4 hours |
| P2 | Major feature degraded | 4 hours | 1 business day |
| P3 | Minor issue or question | 1 business day | 2 business days |

## Escalation to engineering

1. The support engineer reproduces the issue and gathers tenant ID, timestamps and screenshots.
2. P1: page the engineering on-call through the incident tool immediately.
3. P2: file in the owning team's queue with the "customer-impact" label; the team acknowledges within 4 hours.
4. Patrick Okoye (Support Manager) reviews every P1 within 24 hours.

## Customer data in tickets

Never paste customer credentials or raw logs containing personal data into tickets. Use the secure upload link.
