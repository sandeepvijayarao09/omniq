# Research synthesis: on-call engineers

Researcher: Sebastian Ruiz. 14 interviews across 9 customers, August–September.

## Key findings

1. **The first five minutes decide everything.** Engineers open the timeline from the push alert and decide within minutes whether to escalate.
2. **Mobile is triage, desktop is investigation.** 11 of 14 acknowledge on mobile, then move to a laptop.
3. **Trust is fragile.** Two participants at Stark Logistics muted anomaly alerts after a week of noise in v1.
4. **Summaries would help, if they cite evidence.** Every participant wanted to see why a summary says what it says.

## Recommendations

Prioritize push actions on mobile; show the evidence trail in Nimbus summaries; add a "why did this fire" panel for anomaly incidents.
