# Tide design system

Tide is our design system, owned by Design and implemented by Frontend.

## Principles

1. **Calm by default.** Color is for meaning, not decoration. Red means something is broken.
2. **Readable under stress.** Body text 15px minimum in the product, high contrast, plain words.
3. **Show the evidence.** Every summary links to the signals behind it.

## Components

Buttons, inputs, tables, the timeline rail, status pills and empty states are in Figma and the `@halcyon/tide` package. Tide 2.0 adds a dense table mode and dark-mode tokens.

## Contributing

Propose new components in #design with the problem, three real screens that need it, and an accessibility note. Ingrid Solberg reviews weekly.
