# Company OKRs, FY26 H2

## Objective 1: Make Pulse the most reliable choice in our market

- KR1: 99.95% ingest availability every month (Aug: 99.71%, Sep: 99.97%).
- KR2: Zero SEV1 incidents caused by configuration changes.
- KR3: Out-of-band alerting path live for all customers by end of Q4.

## Objective 2: Be ready for enterprise and regulated customers

- KR1: SSO/SAML generally available in December.
- KR2: SOC 2 Type II report issued by January.
- KR3: Two healthcare customers live with a signed BAA.

## Objective 3: Grow efficiently

- KR1: $1.1M net new ARR in H2.
- KR2: Gross retention at or above 92%.
- KR3: Cloud cost per host down 15% from Q3.

## Objective 4: Build a company people want to stay at

- KR1: Regretted attrition under 8% annualized.
- KR2: Every manager completes manager training.
- KR3: Engagement survey participation above 85%.
