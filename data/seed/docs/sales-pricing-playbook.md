# Pricing and discount playbook

## List pricing

- Pulse Team: $18 per host per month, up to 200 hosts.
- Pulse Business: $26 per host per month, adds SSO, 13-month retention and audit export.
- Pulse Enterprise: custom; data residency, private deployment, dedicated support.

## Discount guidance

Account executives can approve up to 10% for annual prepay. 10–20% needs the Head of Sales. Anything above 20% needs CFO sign-off and a multi-year commitment.

## Positioning

Lead with time-to-understanding: Pulse turns an incident into a readable timeline in under a minute. Against larger observability suites, we win on price predictability and on teams of 20–300 engineers who do not have a dedicated observability group.

## After the August outage

Be direct about the incident if asked. Share the public postmortem summary and the ADR-007 remediation; do not share internal customer names.
