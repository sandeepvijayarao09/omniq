# Project Driftwood: EU data region (restricted)

## Proposal

Launch Pulse in an EU region (Frankfurt) in Q2 FY27 to meet data-residency requirements from Globex Financial's EU entity and four other prospects.

## Cost

$1.1M in the first year: infrastructure $640,000, two SRE hires, legal and compliance work. Break-even at about $1.4M in EU-resident ARR, which the pipeline supports by the end of FY27.

## Decision needed

Board approval in the December meeting. Rafael Mendoza and Marcus Whitfield are co-owners. Do not mention Driftwood to customers until approved.
