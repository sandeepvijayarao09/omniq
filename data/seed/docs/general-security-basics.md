# Security basics for every employee

1. **Use SSO and a hardware key.** Every company app is behind SSO. IT issues two security keys on day one; keep one at home.
2. **Never share credentials.** Not in chat, not in docs, not with IT. IT will never ask for your password.
3. **Lock your screen** whenever you step away. Laptops auto-lock after five minutes.
4. **Report phishing** with the Report button in your email client. If you clicked something, tell Security immediately in #security-help; nobody gets in trouble for reporting.
5. **Customer data stays in approved systems.** Never download customer data to your laptop or paste it into tools outside the approved list. Use Omniq for AI help; it removes sensitive data before any model sees it.
6. **Travel.** Turn on device encryption (it is on by default) and use the VPN on public Wi-Fi.

Security incidents: page Security through the incident tool or email security@halcyonlabs.example. The Security team is led by Rebecca Stein.
