# Expense and travel policy

## The principle

Spend company money like it is your own, and write down why.

## Approvals

- Under $500: no pre-approval; submit the receipt in the expense tool within 30 days.
- $500 to $5,000: manager approval before you spend.
- Over $5,000 or any new software subscription: Finance approval through a procurement request, plus a Security review for anything that touches customer data.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel desk (Workplace team). Economy for flights under six hours; premium economy for longer. Hotels up to $300 per night in major cities. Meals while traveling up to $75 per day. Customer dinners need the account owner's sign-off and the attendee list in the expense note.

## Company cards

Issued to managers and anyone who travels monthly. Card statements reconcile on the 5th of each month; late reconciliations block next month's spend.

## Not reimbursable

Personal entertainment, upgrades you chose, traffic fines and alcohol without a customer present.
