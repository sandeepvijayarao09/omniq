# Halcyon Labs at a glance

Halcyon Labs builds Pulse, an observability platform for mid-market engineering teams. Pulse ingests logs, metrics and traces and turns them into incident timelines a human can read in under a minute. We were founded in 2021, are headquartered in San Francisco, and have 100 employees, about a third of them remote.

## Mission and values

Our mission is to make production calm. We hold ourselves to four values: write it down, default to trust, fix the system not the person, and ship small.

"Write it down" is the one new hires notice first. Decisions live in ADRs, processes live in runbooks, and if it was only said in a meeting it did not happen.

## Business snapshot

About 340 customers, mostly companies with 20–300 engineers. Plans: Pulse Team, Pulse Business and Pulse Enterprise. Revenue grew 31% year over year. Our largest segments are fintech, logistics and healthcare.

## Current priorities (FY26 H2)

1. Reliability: hold 99.95% availability for Pulse ingest after the August incident.
2. Enterprise readiness: SSO/SAML, audit export and data residency for regulated customers.
3. Efficiency: bring cloud spend back under plan after the Q3 overrun.
