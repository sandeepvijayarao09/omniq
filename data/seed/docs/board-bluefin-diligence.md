# Project Bluefin: diligence memo (restricted)

Prepared for the board by Helena Brandt and Marcus Whitfield.

## The target

A 14-person log-analytics startup with a fast columnar query engine and about $1.6M ARR. Its engine benchmarks 3–5x faster than our current log search on large tenants.

## Rationale

Bring log search in-house instead of building it over 18 months, and add a strong infrastructure team. Revenue overlap with our customer base is small.

## Terms under discussion

Up to $18M: 60% stock, 40% cash, with a 30% retention pool for the founding engineers over three years.

## Risks

Integration distraction during the SOC 2 audit; the founders' retention; and a code-quality review that is still in progress.

## Next steps

Technical diligence completes October 31. Final recommendation to the board in November. Need-to-know only: CEO, CTO, CFO, General Counsel.
