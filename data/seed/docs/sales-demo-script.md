# Demo script: "the first five minutes of an incident"

Owner: Hiroshi Nakamura. Runtime: 25 minutes.

1. **Set the scene (2 min).** It's 2 a.m., a push alert fires. Open it on the phone.
2. **Timeline (8 min).** Show how Pulse assembled the deploy, the error spike and the trace that explains it. Point out the "what changed" rail.
3. **Anomaly detection (5 min).** Show one grouped anomaly instead of 200 alerts. Mention noise is down to 11% in beta.
4. **Enterprise readiness (5 min).** SSO/SAML (beta now, GA in December), role-based access, retention controls.
5. **Pricing and close (5 min).** Per-host pricing, no ingest overages. Offer a 14-day proof of value with a success plan.

Never demo against a real customer tenant. Use the "Acme Demo" sandbox.
