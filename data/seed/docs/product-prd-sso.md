# PRD: SSO/SAML

Owner: Ben Carter. Engineering: Platform (Priya Raman's team). Design: Mira Kapoor.

## Why

SSO is the most common blocker in enterprise security reviews: 23 of 41 lost enterprise deals in the last year cited it. Umbrella Health and Pinecrest Bank require it to sign.

## Scope

- SAML 2.0 and OIDC with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace.
- Just-in-time provisioning, with SCIM in a follow-up.
- Enforce SSO per organization, with a break-glass admin.

## Success metrics

- 30% of Business customers enable SSO within 90 days.
- Security-review cycle time for enterprise deals down by two weeks.

## Timeline

Private beta with six customers now; general availability in December.
