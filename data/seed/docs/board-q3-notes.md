# Board meeting notes, Q3 FY26 (restricted)

Attendees: Jordan Ellis (CEO), the CFO, three directors.

## Financials

Revenue grew 31% year over year. OpEx was 12% over plan, driven by cloud costs after the August incident. Runway remains above 30 months.

## Project Bluefin

The board approved continuing diligence on Project Bluefin, the proposed acquisition of a log-analytics startup, at a valuation of up to $18M. Jordan Ellis to bring a final recommendation in November. Bluefin is strictly need-to-know; do not discuss outside the executive team.

## Project Nimbus

Nimbus, the AI-assisted incident summarizer, is approved for a private beta with Globex Financial in Q1.

## Risks discussed

Concentration: Globex Financial is 14% of ARR. Reliability: a second SEV1 within two quarters would put the Globex Financial expansion at risk.
