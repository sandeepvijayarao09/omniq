# Your first two weeks

## Day 1

Pick up your laptop from Operations at the front desk. The office is at 500 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 300. Tom Becker runs office operations and can help with badges, desks and parking.

Guest Wi-Fi for visitors: network "Halcyon-Guest", password: TidePool-2026. Employees use the managed "Halcyon" network with SSO.

## Week 1

- Complete security training in the HR portal (about 40 minutes).
- Read the Company Overview and the Acceptable Use of AI Tools policy.
- Engineers: get access to the staging cluster via an IT ticket, then read the Production Deploy Runbook and the latest ADRs.
- Sales: shadow two discovery calls and read the Pricing and Discount Playbook.

## Week 2

Ship something small. Every engineer deploys to production in their second week with an onboarding buddy. Ask Omniq anything first; ask your buddy second.

## Buddies

New engineers on Platform are paired with Maya Chen or another senior engineer. New sales hires are paired by Sofia Alvarez.
