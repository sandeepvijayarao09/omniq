# IT help desk guide

## Getting help

File a ticket in #it-help or the IT portal. P1 (you cannot work): response in 30 minutes during business hours. Everything else: same business day.

## Common requests

- **Access to an app:** request it in the IT portal; your manager approves, then IT provisions through SSO. Access to production systems also needs approval from the system owner's team lead.
- **New laptop or replacement:** turnaround is two business days in the Bay Area, four for remote employees.
- **Lost or stolen device:** report within one hour to #it-help and Security. IT will remotely wipe it.
- **Omniq access level:** your Omniq role (Basic, Pro, Manager, Admin, Executive) follows your job. If you need broader data access for a project, your manager requests it in the IT portal with an end date.
