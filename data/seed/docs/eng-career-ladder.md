# Engineering career ladder

| Level | Title | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Software Engineer I | Delivers well-defined tasks with guidance |
| E2 | Software Engineer II | Owns features end to end |
| E3 | Senior Software Engineer | Owns a system; leads projects across a team |
| E4 | Staff Software Engineer | Sets technical direction across teams |
| E5 | Principal Engineer | Shapes company-wide architecture |

## Promotions

Promotions happen in the January and July cycles. The manager writes the case with the engineer; a calibration group of engineering managers reviews it, chaired by Nadia Petrova. Evidence beats tenure: link the design docs, incidents and reviews that show the next level's scope.

## Management track

Engineering managers are typically E3+ engineers who chose to lead people. Moving between tracks is normal and supported.
