# Benefits overview 2026

## Health

Medical, dental and vision through our carrier, with 100% of employee premiums and 80% of dependent premiums covered. Two medical plans: a PPO and a high-deductible plan with a company HSA contribution of $1,500 per year ($3,000 for family coverage). Mental health: 12 free therapy sessions per year through our EAP, then covered under the medical plan.

## Retirement

401(k) with a 4% match, vesting immediately. Enroll in the benefits portal within 30 days of your start date.

## Money for your work and life

- Home office stipend: $750 once, plus $50 per month for internet.
- Learning budget: $1,500 per year for courses, books and conferences. Ask your manager, then submit in the expense tool.
- Wellness: $60 per month, usable for gyms, classes or equipment.

## Leave

See the Time Off and Leave Policy for PTO, sick leave and 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
