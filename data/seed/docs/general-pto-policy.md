# Time off and leave

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next year. Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.

## Parental leave

All parents, regardless of gender or how they became a parent, receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months. Tell your manager and HR at least 8 weeks before your planned start date where possible.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not capped for short illnesses. For absences longer than five working days, HR will ask for documentation, which is kept confidential and stored only in the HR system.

## Company holidays

We observe 11 company holidays plus a winter shutdown between December 24 and January 1.
