# Globex Financial MSA: key terms

Prepared by Samuel Adeyemi (Commercial Counsel). Privileged and confidential.

- **Term:** three years from March 2025, auto-renewing annually.
- **Fees:** $612,000 per year, with an expansion to Enterprise under negotiation (+$310,000).
- **SLA:** 99.9% monthly availability; service credits of 10% of monthly fees per 0.1% below the SLA, capped at 50%. The August outage triggered credits of $28,500.
- **Termination:** Globex may terminate for convenience with 90 days' notice after the second SEV1 in any rolling 12-month window. This is why a second SEV1 is a board-level risk.
- **Data:** DPA signed; EU data residency requested for the expansion (depends on Project Driftwood).
- **Liability cap:** 12 months of fees, uncapped for data-protection breaches.
