# Acceptable use of AI tools

Omniq is Halcyon Labs' sanctioned AI assistant. If you are using Omniq the way it is designed, you are inside policy: it only shows you documents your role permits, strips personal and confidential data before any model call, and logs every interaction for compliance.

## Do

- Use Omniq for drafting, summarizing, research across internal docs, meeting prep and code questions.
- Upload documents to your personal workspace freely; upload to shared categories only if you own the content.
- Review AI output before sending it to a customer. You are accountable for what you send.

## Do not

- Paste customer data, source code, credentials, financials or board material into public AI services (consumer chatbots, browser extensions, free online tools).
- Try to make Omniq reveal documents outside your role. Attempts are logged.

## Why

In 2025 an employee at a peer company pasted an acquisition model into a public chatbot. We would rather give everyone a safe tool than ask people not to use AI at all.

Report concerns to the IT administrator or security@halcyonlabs.example.
