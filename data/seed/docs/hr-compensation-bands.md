# Compensation bands FY26 (confidential)

Salary bands (base pay ranges) for every level. Bands are reviewed each January against market data for the San Francisco Bay Area. Remote employees outside the Bay Area are paid at 90% of the band.

## Engineering, product and design

- I level (Software Engineer I): salary band $118,000–$140,000 base
- II level (Software Engineer II): salary band $140,000–$168,000 base
- Senior level (Senior Software Engineer): salary band $168,000–$200,000 base
- Staff level (Staff Software Engineer): salary band $205,000–$245,000 base
- Manager level (Engineering Manager): salary band $190,000–$235,000 base

## Go-to-market

Account executives: $90,000–$130,000 base with a 50/50 split; enterprise AEs up to $150,000 base. SDRs: $62,000–$75,000 base plus $20,000 variable. Customer success managers: $105,000–$140,000 base plus 10% bonus on net retention.

## Equity

New-hire grants vest over four years with a one-year cliff. Refresh grants are awarded in the March cycle for "Exceeds" and above.

## Process

Offers outside band need approval from Monique Dupree and the department's executive. Off-cycle raises are frozen until the January review except for promotions.
