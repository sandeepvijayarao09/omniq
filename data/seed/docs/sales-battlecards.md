# Competitive battlecards

## Against the large observability suites

**Where they win:** breadth of features and brand. **Where we win:** predictable pricing (per host, no ingest surprises), timelines that a new on-call engineer can read in a minute, and a team that answers in hours. **Landmine to set:** ask how much their last bill varied month to month.

## Against open-source self-hosted stacks

**Where they win:** cost on paper and control. **Where we win:** total cost once you count the two engineers who run it, plus anomaly detection and timelines out of the box. **Landmine:** ask who gets paged when the observability stack itself is down.

## Against point tools (logging-only or APM-only)

**Where we win:** one timeline across logs, metrics, traces and deploys. Consolidation saves most customers 20–35%.

## Handling the August outage

Acknowledge it plainly, share the public postmortem summary and explain the ADR-007 and ADR-012 fixes. Never name affected customers.
