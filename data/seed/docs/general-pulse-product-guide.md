# Pulse product guide

Pulse is an observability platform that turns telemetry into incident timelines.

## Core concepts

- **Sources:** where telemetry comes from: agents, OpenTelemetry collectors, cloud integrations.
- **Signals:** logs, metrics and traces after ingest and indexing.
- **Detectors:** threshold rules and anomaly detection (v2 in beta) that open incidents.
- **Timelines:** the heart of Pulse. When an incident opens, Pulse assembles the relevant signals, deploys and config changes into one readable story.

## Plans

- **Pulse Team:** up to 200 hosts, 30-day retention.
- **Pulse Business:** adds SSO, 13-month retention, audit export (January) and anomaly detection.
- **Pulse Enterprise:** data residency, private deployment and dedicated support.

## What's coming

SSO/SAML for all paid plans in December. Audit export in January. AI incident summaries are in development and not yet announced; don't discuss them with customers.
