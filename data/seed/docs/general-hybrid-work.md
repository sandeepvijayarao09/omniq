# Hybrid and remote work

## Defaults

Bay Area employees work from the Harbor Boulevard office on Tuesdays and Thursdays ("anchor days"). Remote employees visit for one planning week per quarter, with travel paid by the company.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 9:00–13:00 Pacific. Outside core hours, default to asynchronous communication: write it down, link the doc, and don't expect an instant reply.

## Working from another country

Up to 30 days per year from another country with manager approval. Longer stays need People and Legal approval for tax and immigration reasons.

## Equipment

IT ships a laptop and peripherals before your start date. Use only company-managed devices for company data. Personal devices may access email and chat through the managed app only.
